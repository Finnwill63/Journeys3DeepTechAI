The Journeys 3 approach to mentorship

About the Deep Tech and AI Mentor Network

Twelve teams, forty-six mentors. This network holds a structured profile of each, written to one shared vocabulary of sectors and capabilities, so that what a team needs can be matched to the mentors best placed to meet it.

How we work

Teams make the first move. After kick-off, you contact the mentor. Don’t wait to be approached.

Every few weeks, not weekly. Online or in person is the mentor’s call.

Validation is the thread running through the whole program. Keep testing the idea, consistently, and get outside your comfort zone doing it; cold-connect with the people you think will buy, and find out what they actually say.

Come to meetings with something specific. A mentor’s time is much more valuable thinking about real questions, as opposed to just a status update.

The initial coding is a starting point based on the information I have available. Every profile here was built from public material (with a little help from the cohort teams’ presentations), and it will be wrong in places. Tell me and I’ll fix it — that goes for mentors reading their own entry and teams reading theirs.

Each team’s initial issues is a hypothesis. I’ve written a first guess at what each team needs, from the application material. It’s a guess. It gets updated as the program evolves.

This site will also evolve! I am not happy with the usability yet, will continue to develop. I think it is better than sharing spreadsheets, etc. One key upgrade is that I will integrate better with our Dealum platform in the future.
