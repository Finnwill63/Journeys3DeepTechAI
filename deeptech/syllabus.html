Annotated Syllabus

A 16-Week Path to a Fundable Venture

31 Aug – 10 Dec 2026. Each week is annotated so you always know why it's there and what it asks of you.

Journeys 3 turns founders and co-founders of deep-tech and AI ventures - whatever their background - into operators who can raise. It is built on one idea: every major decision is driven by evidence - LOIs, pilots, JDAs, grants, revenue - not opinion. Here, progress is measured in evidence, not activity.

How the calendar runs. A networking evening on Mon 31 Aug opens the program; Tue 1 Sep is a combined, all-tracks session on the entrepreneurial mindset, followed by a deep-tech breakout. Two Tuesdays have no session - 13 Oct (Finnish fall break) and 17 Nov (Slush) - and their topics are merged into the sessions before them, so nothing is cut and Demo Day holds on 10 Dec.

Two tracks. Track A - VC-speed SaaS / API / B2C. Track B - long-horizon deep tech & regulated AI. You get a default track at kickoff and can re-frame at Checkpoint 1.

Your week, every week. A pre-read under an hour before Tuesday; the Tuesday-evening session, 17:00–20:00, where you apply the ideas to your own venture, with mentors on hand; a mentor 1:1 matched to your current riskiest assumption; and real execution between sessions.

Two checkpoints and a Demo Day. At Weeks 8 and 13 you make honest decisions on the evidence - kill, pivot, or double down, then whether to raise now or wait. Week 16 is proof in public.

How to read each week

Why now. why this week sits where it does, and what it unlocks.

By the end, you can. the concrete capability you'll walk away with.

Connects to. how the week links to the last one and to the arc toward fundable.

Cases. the teaching cases - real companies and decision-forcing composites.

Pre-read / You'll produce. what to read beforehand, and the evidence you'll create in your Team Record.

What we expect of you
  • Arrive at each Tuesday session having done the pre-read - the session assumes it.
  • Update your Team Record every Monday (five minutes). This weekly commit is the heartbeat of the program.
  • Produce evidence, not activity - every week ends with a specific commitment you'll be held to.
  • Be honest about what the data says, including when it says stop. An early, cheap “no” is a win.

Part I · Foundation Weeks 1–8

Week 131 Aug – 1 Sep - Kickoff - Entrepreneurial Mindset + Founder Diagnostics
Foundation · Combined + breakout

Why now.The program opens with a networking evening (Mon 31 Aug) and a combined, all-tracks session on the entrepreneurial mindset (Tue 1 Sep). You arrive as a founder or co-founder - technical, commercial, or domain expert; the company needs a CEO who runs on evidence.

By the end, you can.Think in assumptions and evidence rather than features, name your venture's riskiest assumptions, and - in the deep-tech breakout after the plenary - stand up your Team Record and a provisional track.

Connects to.Seeds the Assumption Map that drives Weeks 2–7 and sets the weekly rhythm you'll run all program.

Cases.ICEYE (real) - the Aalto radar team who sold Earth observation before the constellation existed; HelioAssay (decision-forcing) - perfect-and-publish vs. sell the 85%-accurate device now.

Pre-read.~55 min - the founder-to-CEO shift and “sell first, build later.”

You'll produce.The Team Record live, an initial riskiest-assumption map (commercial / technical / regulatory), a provisional track, and 5+ booked discovery conversations.

Week 28 Sep - Customer & Partner Discovery
Foundation

Why now.Whatever you've already built, you need the truth about who actually has the problem and whether they'll act on it. Sharp discovery redirects months of effort.

By the end, you can.Run interviews that surface truth (the Mom Test, Jobs-to-be-Done) and log every conversation as evidence.

Connects to.Fills the first rows of your Evidence Ledger and sharpens the assumptions you'll test next week.

Cases.Varjo (real) - discovery revealed the real buyer wasn't the consumer but the enterprise; CortexRead (decision-forcing) - glowing interviews, zero commitments - believe the praise or force a real yes?

Pre-read.~50 min - the Mom Test, Jobs-to-be-Done, and partner/pilot discovery.

You'll produce.8–10 logged interviews and a first, evidence-based cut of your ideal customer or partner.

Week 315 Sep - Lean Canvas & Risk Mapping
Foundation

Why now.With discovery in hand, you lock what could actually kill the venture - so you spend scarce runway on the right test, not the fun one.

By the end, you can.Rank your 5–7 riskiest assumptions and tag each as commercial, technical, or regulatory.

Connects to.The tagged map is what routes your mentors to you and sets the experiments you design in Week 4.

Cases.IQM (real) - mapping which risk in a daunting stack actually kills you; TerraNova Materials (decision-forcing) - one test, three unknowns: prove scale, demand, or regulatory admissibility first?

Pre-read.~37 min - the Lean Canvas and the riskiest-assumption test.

You'll produce.A finalised, tagged Assumption Map in your Team Record.

Week 422 Sep - Experiment & Offer Design
Foundation

Why now.Assumptions are only useful once you test them - and the test that matters most is whether someone will commit.

By the end, you can.Design experiments whose results would change your mind, and an offer that elicits LOIs, pilots, or pre-orders.

Connects to.Launches the experiments whose evidence you'll present at Checkpoint 1.

Cases.Norsepower (real) - a paid, independently-verified pilot became the offer and the sales asset; KaironWeld (decision-forcing) - free trials wide vs. one instrumented paid pilot.

Pre-read.~38 min - experiment design, pre-selling, and offer design.

You'll produce.2–3 designed experiments and a live offer made to real prospects.

Week 529 Sep - MVP / PoC v0.1
Foundation

Why now.Many of you have already shipped. The test isn't whether it works - it's whether what you've built proves someone will commit. Scope to exactly that.

By the end, you can.Scope an MVP or PoC around a real “value moment” and a success metric.

Connects to.Turns your offer into something testable, and (Track B) begins your technical-milestone evidence.

Cases.Oura (real) - from a raw biosignal firehose to one Readiness number people pay for; Helix Foundry (decision-forcing) - flashy design-anything demo vs. one narrow paid validation.

Pre-read.~36 min - why an MVP is not a prototype, the value moment, and deep-tech PoC framing.

You'll produce.MVP / PoC v0.1, shipped to your first users or buyers.

Weeks 6–76 Oct - Usability & Desirability + Commitment Funnels
Foundation · Merged session

Why now.A working thing is not a wanted thing - so you test desirability and willingness - and then you manufacture commitment at volume and open the story that will one day carry the raise. Fall break falls on 13 Oct, so these two topics run together here.

By the end, you can.Measure willingness to pay or pilot and iterate to a stronger v0.2; run a commitment funnel with numeric targets and draft your core narrative.

Connects to.Produces the willingness signal and the first signed commitments that Checkpoint 1 reviews on 20 Oct.

Cases.Live (two): Framery (real) - desirability read from real buying, not applause; VeriSense (decision-forcing) - commitment-funnel breadth vs. depth before a checkpoint. Also in the case pack: Tundra Thermal, Aiven.

Pre-read.~55 min (merged & trimmed) - desirability & willingness-to-pay tests, plus designing commitment funnels.

You'll produce.MVP v0.2 with a moving willingness metric, an active funnel with targets, your first signed commitments, and a first draft of your story.

13 Oct - no session · Finnish fall break - Use the break for the Weeks 6–7 fieldwork: run your willingness tests and push for commitments so the evidence is ready for Checkpoint 1. Optional mentor 1:1s on request.
Week 820 Oct - Checkpoint 1 - Kill / Pivot / Double Down
Foundation · Gate

Why now.Eight weeks of evidence, one honest decision. This is where opinions lose to data.

By the end, you can.Make and defend a kill, pivot, or double-down decision on the evidence - and reset your roadmap.

Connects to.Your Weeks 1–7 evidence becomes a synthesis; the decision sets the shape of the rest of the program.

Prepare.Not a reading - build your Checkpoint 1 synthesis: a clear view of your Team Record (assumptions, evidence, funnel vs. targets, your ask).

You'll produce.A decision, a confirmed track, and a reset roadmap - logged in your Decisions Log.

Part II · Specialisation Weeks 9–16

Week 927 Oct - Narrative & Story
Specialisation

Why now.With real traction behind you, you can build a narrative that is proof, not spin - the story an investor's committee will actually read.

By the end, you can.Tell an evidence-grounded venture story and position it clearly in the market.

Connects to.Builds on your Week 7 draft and the program's Messaging Spine, and feeds every investor conversation from here.

Cases.Solar Foods (real) - a bold “food from air” narrative nailed to dated regulatory proof; Helio Catalytica (decision-forcing) - the honest “better-X” story vs. the fundable “promised land.”

Pre-read.~47 min - strategic narrative, positioning, and the IC-memo logic.

You'll produce.An IC-memo-ready narrative and positioning.

Week 103 Nov - GTM & Sales Motion
Specialisation

Why now.You choose how you'll win the market on purpose - the motion, the 90-day plan, the pricing - rather than by default.

By the end, you can.Select a primary go-to-market motion and draft a 90-day plan and a pricing model.

Connects to.Turns your narrative into a route to revenue, and sets up the live pipeline you build next week.

Cases.Wolt (real) - the disciplined city-by-city playbook that beat blitz-scaling rivals; Aavo Diagnostics (decision-forcing) - founder-led sales vs. OEM channel vs. land-and-expand.

Pre-read.~58 min - GTM motions, pricing, and design partners (Track B).

You'll produce.A primary motion, a 90-day roadmap, and a pricing model.

Weeks 11–1210 Nov - Sales / BD Pipeline + Team & Operating System
Specialisation · Merged session

Why now.You stand up a real, forecastable pipeline and, in the same session, build the company behind it - the co-founder charter, hiring scorecard, and operating cadence investors underwrite. Slush falls on 17 Nov, so these two topics run together here.

By the end, you can.Run a CRM-backed pipeline with honest qualification and a Sales/BD Playbook; write a co-founder charter and hiring scorecard and set your operating cadence.

Connects to.Converts your GTM plan into live deals and de-risks the team - both scrutinised at Checkpoint 2 on 24 Nov.

Cases.Live (two): RELEX (real) - disciplined qualification in long enterprise cycles; Näkö Robotics (decision-forcing) - a 50/50 co-founder deadlock before a key hire. Also in the case pack: Ferrosen Materials, Supercell.

Pre-read.~55 min (merged & trimmed) - pipeline discipline and qualification, plus the co-founder charter and scorecard hiring.

You'll produce.A live pipeline and Sales/BD Playbook v0.1; a co-founder charter, one hiring scorecard, and your operating cadence.

17 Nov - no session · Slush field week - Attend Slush as a live GTM lab: target a set number of investor and BD conversations, apply your funnel and pipeline discipline, and log every outcome in your Team Record. This fieldwork feeds Checkpoint 2.
Week 1324 Nov - Checkpoint 2 - Fundability Review
Specialisation · Gate

Why now.The second honest gate: is this fundable now - and should you raise now, or wait for the milestone that changes the terms?

By the end, you can.Assess your traction, unit economics, and technical progress, and set a funding strategy.

Connects to.Your Weeks 9–12 work and your Slush fieldwork become a fundability case; the decision shapes the run to Demo Day.

Prepare.Not a reading - build your fundability synthesis: traction, unit economics, milestones, Slush outcomes, and funding options.

You'll produce.A funding-strategy decision - raise now vs. delay, and the shape of the round.

Week 141 Dec - Legal, Cap Table & Data Room
Specialisation

Why now.Fundability is built, not bolted on. You clean the structure so nothing stalls an investor mid-diligence.

By the end, you can.Present a clean cap table, an ESOP philosophy, IP/regulatory clarity, and a mini data room.

Connects to.Removes the diligence killers before Investor Readiness next week.

Cases.Nanoform (real) - patents plus a regulator's manufacturing certificate made the data room fundable; CryoLumen (decision-forcing) - a university-IP royalty that reads as a diligence killer.

Pre-read.~27 min - cap tables, ESOP in Europe, the data room, and deep-tech diligence.

You'll produce.A mini data room and a cleaned-up cap table, IP, and ESOP position.

Week 158 Dec - Investor Readiness
Specialisation

Why now.You assemble and pressure-test everything an investor needs, and seal the Evidence Pack you've been building since Week 1.

By the end, you can.Present an IC memo and financial plan, design your round, and hold up under mock diligence.

Connects to.Turns fifteen weeks of evidence into an investor-legible package for Demo Day.

Cases.IQM (real) - layering VC, non-dilutive and strategic capital across a decade-long build; FennoWatt Materials (decision-forcing) - raise now, delay for a 2× mark, or bridge?

Pre-read.~56 min - the IC memo, round design, and the European raise.

You'll produce.An IC memo, a financial plan, a sealed Evidence Pack, and a tightened ask.

Week 1610 Dec (Thu) - Demo Day
Specialisation · Milestone

Why now.Proof, in public. You pitch to investors and corporates and open real, structured follow-up.

By the end, you can.Deliver a credible, evidence-backed pitch and run a disciplined follow-up process.

Connects to.Your Team Record becomes the investor-facing view - and the habits you've built keep running after the program ends.

Prepare.Not a reading - final rehearsal, with your Evidence Pack ready.

You'll produce.Your pitch, and a live investor follow-up pipeline.

How we work operating principles

Sell First, Build Later.Let commitment lead: sell ahead of your next build, and let signed proof - not conviction - pull the roadmap.
Customer & Partner > Product.Decisions anchor in buyer behaviour, not technical elegance.
An MVP is not a prototype.A prototype proves the tech works; an MVP proves someone will pay or commit.
Time Is Blood.Every week without new evidence is a week closer to the end.
Evidence > Opinions.Build, pivot, hire, raise - all driven by numbers and artefacts.
Fundability by Design.Legal, cap table, and data room are built in parallel, so the raise isn't a scramble.

What you'll leave with

An investor-legible venture - backed by a real Evidence Pack, a narrative grounded in traction, a clean and fundable structure, and, above all, a clear-eyed decision about your company's future made on data rather than hope. Some of you will leave investment-ready; some with a sharper long-horizon strategy and non-dilutive momentum; some having learned, early and cheaply, what shouldn't be built. What no one leaves with is a false sense of progress.
