CMS proposes Medicare reimbursement for CT-based fracture-risk AI ↗

In its proposed CY2027 OPPS rule, CMS placed CT-based fracture-risk AI diagnostics into a new 'Software as a Medical Service' framework, temporarily assigning them to New Technology APCs, a significant signal on reimbursing imaging-analysis AI.

Why it mattersA reimbursement pathway for imaging-derived fracture-risk AI directly de-risks the US commercial model for Ossify Health's multi-modal fracture-risk reports.

2026-08-04Research

Deep-learning model spots osteoporosis on routine CT with high accuracy ↗

NYU Langone and UCSF researchers, publishing in Radiology, built a deep-learning model that opportunistically detects low bone density on CT scans done for other reasons, validated across 538,000+ exams, with cross-scanner harmonization.

Why it mattersValidates the opportunistic, multi-source imaging approach at the core of Ossify Health's fracture-risk reporting and strengthens the clinical case for CT-derived bone signals.

2026-07-29Market

NICE publishes updated osteoporosis risk-assessment guideline NG259 ↗

NICE published NG259, updating CG146 with clearer criteria on who should receive fracture-risk assessment, recommendations for vertebral fracture assessment during DXA in older adults, and streamlined direct-to-DXA referral pathways.

Why it mattersUpdated risk-assessment and VFA guidance reshapes the clinical workflow Ossify Health's structured reports must map to for adoption in bone-health pathways.

2026-06-18Research

AI-derived BMD from X-rays matches DXA for fracture prediction ↗

A 10-year real-world cohort study in Osteoporosis International found AI-derived bone mineral density from standard radiographs predicted vertebral (AUC 0.704) and hip fractures (0.716) comparably to DXA, suggesting screening without dedicated DXA equipment.

Why it mattersEvidence that AI can extract DXA-comparable fracture-risk signal from routine images underpins Ossify Health's premise of fusing imaging with genetic and lab data.

2026-06-05Funding

Promedius raises 21.5B won for AI osteoporosis screening ↗

Korean startup Promedius raised 21.5 billion won (cumulative funding above 35 billion won) from investors including Daewoong Pharmaceutical and Naver. Its 'Osteo Signal' product screens osteoporosis risk from chest X-rays in about three seconds.

Why it mattersA large raise for AI-based opportunistic bone screening confirms active investor appetite in exactly Ossify Health's category and signals competitive dynamics.
